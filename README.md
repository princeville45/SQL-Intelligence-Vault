# SQL Intelligence Vault

Professional-grade SQL patterns for business intelligence, revenue analytics, and operations reporting. Built by a Statistical Business Architect.

This repository is a curated vault of SQL solutions designed for real-world business challenges. Unlike textbook exercises, these patterns focus on the complexity of production data—handling messy time series, complex customer lifecycles, and operational bottlenecks.

## Core Competencies Covered:
. **Advanced Window Functions**: LAG, RANK, SUM OVER, NTILE for trend and distribution analysis.
. **CTEs (Common Table Expressions)**: Chaining logic for readable, modular analytics pipelines.
. **Revenue Analytics**: Cohort retention, Churn detection, MoM growth, and LTV calculation.
. **Operations Intelligence**: Inventory velocity, FIFO valuation, and supplier performance metrics.
. **Data Engineering Patterns**: JSON extraction, recursive hierarchies, and dynamic date filtering.

Designed for high-impact decision-making.
